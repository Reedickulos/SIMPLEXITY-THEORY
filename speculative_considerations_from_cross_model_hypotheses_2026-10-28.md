## 1. Identity and observational partition

**Question:** Can two apparently independent observed entities arise from a context-dependent partition of one intact eight-dimensional relational structure, without any loss of intrinsic information?

**Proposed diagnostic:** Define a context-indexed labeling or partition I_c(G). Compare I_c1(G) and I_c2(G) while preserving G. Explicitly state what a context is and how it is selected from intrinsic data.

**Failure criterion:** Every admissible context gives the same partition, or the proposed change requires altering the underlying relations.

**Boundary:** This does not derive entanglement, measurement outcomes, Born probabilities, Bell violation, or no-signaling.

## 2. Golden partner directionality versus permutation

**Question:** Is the important invariant the directional relationship between paired roots, rather than a naïve claim that swapping partners is an isometry?

**Candidate mathematics for independent investigation:** For complementary eight-dimensional projectors P_A and P_B, L=phi P_A-phi^(-1)P_B has L^2=L+I. For a certified inner-to-outer matching p, its full two-way extension J differs from the global linear map L on the outer half.

**Diagnostic:** Compare directional, signed, and reciprocal relations of root pairs, and their four cross-Gram entries. Test whether any intrinsic symmetry preserves L, J, both, or neither. Do not equate these without proof.

**Boundary:** The algebraic identities are conditional on the projectors; any claimed physical directionality is speculative.

## 3. Intrinsic order-three symmetry and pairing compatibility

**Question:** Which order-three Gram automorphisms arise intrinsically, and how do they act on the certified golden partner map?

**Diagnostic:** Discover automorphisms from G without representation labels. Archive permutation, cycle structure, fixed roots, eigenspace dimensions, and conjugation of J. Distinguish stabilizing J from transporting J to a different certified pairing.

**Failure criterion:** No nontrivial order-three automorphism has the proposed interaction; or apparent compatibility is only a tautological consequence of choosing a power of the same Coxeter element.

**Boundary:** An order-three automorphism is not, by itself, triality.

## 4. Orbit or family of partner maps

**Question:** Might an invariant *family* of golden partner maps be more fundamental than any individual map?

**Diagnostic:** For intrinsically discovered automorphisms t, enumerate the conjugation orbit {t^k J t^(-k)}. Check which maps are valid certified Coxeter partnerings and whether the orbit closes in length 1, 3, or another divisor of the order.

**Failure criterion:** Conjugates fail the certified pairing conditions or are only relabelings without a substantive invariant.

## 5. Comparison with established triality, only after blind derivation

## 6. Direction, relation, and observation

**Question:** Could observationally assigned 'separation' or 'object identity' be sensitive to directional relational information that is invariant in the underlying eight-dimensional structure?

**Diagnostic:** Specify an intrinsic map from relation/direction data to a context-dependent observation. Prove which invariants survive and which distinctions become inaccessible. Check whether a non-injective observation map, rather than literal destruction of information, suffices.

**Boundary:** No physical spacetime metric, signaling law, quantum measurement rule, or entanglement prediction has been derived.
