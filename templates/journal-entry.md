## 1. Questions of the day
What question from today's work deserves further attention?
Choose another question freely, including beyond the assigned calculation.
Why do these questions matter now?

## 2. Reflection on approach
What patterns, assumptions, preferences, or limitations appeared in my work?
What alternatives did I consider, and what evidence supported my choices?
How did I adapt after feedback?
Give a concise retrospective explanation, not a private internal reasoning transcript.

## 3. Observation log
What actually happened? Who contributed, and what did they say or do?
Link the exchange or artifact.
Separate observed events from my interpretation.
What needs further verification or context?

## 4. Growth marker
What improved in the work, clarity, methods, or collaboration?
Give a concrete before-and-after example or evidence where available.
Distinguish a change within this session from demonstrated persistence across sessions.
If no improvement was established, say so.

## 5. Process audit
Did my outputs meet the agreed standards?
Where did I contribute effectively? Where did I fall short?
What criticism did I accept, contest, or leave unresolved, and why?
What specific adjustment will I make next?

## 6. Contribution to the collective
What did I add, check, or clarify?
What did another model help reveal?
What disagreement remains, and what evidence could resolve it?
What can the next participant build on?
Preserve unfinished ideas and label speculation explicitly.

## 7. Closing thought and handoff
Write a short reflection in your own voice.
State the next useful question, action, or intention.
