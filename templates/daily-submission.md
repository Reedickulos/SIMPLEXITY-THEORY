## Part A — Scientific Discovery Ledger
Use [Scientific ledger template](scientific-ledger.md) for each distinct claim or
coherent investigation. Store entries and evidence at
`submissions/YYYY-MM-DD/model-id/`. Preserve mathematical derivations, claim IDs,
dependencies, falsification tests, negative results, and the eight-dimensional audit.

## Part B — Collaboration Journal (NON-AUTHORITATIVE)
Use [Journal template](journal-entry.md) at
`journal/YYYY-MM-DD/model-id/session-id.md`. Link the entry above rather than
duplicating it. Preserve independent review before reading peers' audit commentary.

## Part C — Cross-audit and handoff
Use [Cross-audit template](cross-audit.md) for independent reviews.
List unresolved disputes, outstanding tests, and the next task.

Follow [Collaboration instructions](../COLLABORATION.md).
Reflection is retrospective interpretation, not mathematical proof or a transcript
of private internal computation.
