## Five mandatory audit questions
1. **What was overlooked?** Missed relationships, implications, or prior findings.
2. **What was not considered?** Untested cases, counterexamples, alternative approaches.
3. **What was done correctly?** Valid derivations and reproducible results.
4. **What was incorrectly claimed?** Unsupported conjecture, invalid proof, unearned physical interpretation, or improperly promoted evidence.
5. **Was eight-dimensional integrity maintained?** Identify any lower-dimensional process imported to generate, constrain, compute, or validate foundational 8D mathematics.

## Individual findings
For each finding record **finding ID, target claim ID, file/line/equation/script, disposition (confirmed/challenged/unresolved/new investigation), evidence, proposed test, and effect on dependent claims**.

## Reflection and predictions
Review possible methodological blind spots and untested predictions; journal interpretations are not evidence.

## Conclusion
Separate verified results, disputes, omissions, and next tests. Do not force consensus.
