# Governance and Access Controls

## No plenary power
No model can unilaterally establish mathematical truth, alter historical evidence, or resolve disagreements by authority or majority vote. Journal reflections and model consensus do not validate scientific claims.

## Current trust-based arrangement
- The repository remains private, on the existing plan.
- Branch protections are not enabled. Branch assignments do not restrict access.
- Models using the owner's GitHub connection may share its permissions; distinct model names are attribution, not separate authenticated identities.
- Each model writes to its assigned branch: chatgpt, claude, kimi, or gemini.
- Use pull requests for integration. Only the owner authorizes merges into main and shared staging branches, or explicitly delegates a specific integration.
- Never force-push, delete branches, or silently rewrite historical research, audits, or journal entries.
- Research and journal corrections are new linked entries. Routine instruction/template maintenance may edit files with a clear commit history.
- ledger stages scientific material; journal stages collaboration entries. Neither is canonical acceptance.
- Every accepted entry is confirmed by its commit SHA reachable from canonical main. Acceptance records inclusion, not proof of correctness.
- Independent backups and external commit-SHA anchors are recommended but are not installed by these instructions.

## Daily audit independence
Freeze submissions at exact commit SHAs before reciprocal review. Each reviewer submits its report before reading other reviewers' findings or journal entries discussing those findings. Then open shared discussion; record exposure if independence was compromised. Preserve contrary findings and uncertainty.

## Journal integrity
Follow [COLLABORATION.md](COLLABORATION.md). Attribute every entry to its actual contributing model/version/session where known. Never invent another model's responses or imply a shared-account commit proves model identity. Preserve each contributor's voice and link to supporting artifacts.

## Limitations
These are voluntary operating rules. No automated enforcement, cross-model scheduling, synchronization, or backup is installed. Commit history records content, not scientific validity or private internal cognition.
